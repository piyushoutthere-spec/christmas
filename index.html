The First Christmas

More than two thousand years ago, in the town of Bethlehem, a child was born who would change the course of history. According to Christian tradition, Mary and Joseph had travelled there but finding no suitable place to stay, they took shelter in a humble setting. There, baby Jesus was born and laid in a manger. That quiet night became the beginning of a story remembered across generations. Shepherds came to visit, guided by angels while wise men followed a bright star. WHat begin in a small town eventually became the celebrations of christmas, spreading a message of hope,faith and love throughout the world.
